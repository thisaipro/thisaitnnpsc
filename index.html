The Program

One combined Group 1, 2 & 4 foundation

TNPSC Group 1, Group 2 and Group 4 share close to 70% common ground in General Studies. Instead of preparing separately for each, our combined program builds your foundation once — deeply — and then trains you specifically for whichever exam(s) you're targeting.

What's covered

  • Common Foundation: History, Geography, Indian Polity, Economy, Science & Environment, Aptitude & Mental Ability, Current Affairs, Tamil Language & Culture
  • Group-specific layers: descriptive / Mains-style answer writing (Group 1), Group 2 paper-pattern drills, Group 4 speed & accuracy drills
  • Weekly full-length + topic-wise tests mapped to each group's actual exam pattern

Who this is for

  • Aspirants who haven't decided which group to target yet and want a foundation that keeps every door open
  • Aspirants preparing for more than one group in the same cycle
  • Aspirants who want depth (Group 1/2) but also want Group 4 as a safety net

FAQ

Questions aspirants ask

Is this one combined course, or separate courses for each group?

It's one shared foundation covering the Group 1, 2 & 4 syllabus overlap, with group-specific practice layered on top based on which exam(s) you're targeting.

Do I need to have decided my target group before joining?

No — many students start with the combined foundation and choose their primary target as the exam calendar approaches.

Who is the free UPSC mentorship for?

It's a merit-based, free program for Erode's talented aspirants, guided by candidates who have reached the UPSC interview stage. It's our way of lifting local students toward the national civil-services stage.

Does this program fully cover Group 1 Mains?

The shared foundation covers the General Studies base thoroughly. Group 1 requires additional Mains-specific coaching as the exam approaches — we're upfront that the foundation program alone doesn't fully cover Group 1 Mains.

How does the refund guarantee actually work?

It applies when you meet the attendance, test-completion and study-plan conditions listed in the Progress Guarantee section above. Full terms are provided at enrollment.
