02 What is TNPSC Group 4?

One exam. Multiple government career opportunities.

TNPSC Group IV is one of Tamil Nadu's major recruitment examinations for various Group IV services and posts. For many aspirants, it is an opportunity to enter government service through a focused, competitive examination.

But clearing Group 4 requires more than simply completing the syllabus. You need:

  • Concept clarity
  • Consistent practice
  • Current affairs awareness
  • Speed & accuracy
  • Regular testing
  • Strong revision

That's where a structured preparation system matters.
